Facility and generation update

Faculty: Working days and hours remain the only availability windows. The extra preferred-time controls are removed and migration 9 clears their retired database values. Facility is an optional preference, never permission to ignore facility type, capacity, ownership or conflicts. New faculty use optional prefix, required first and last names, optional middle name, extension and email. Existing display names remain unchanged until their structured name is explicitly edited.

Subjects: Add units separately for lecture, laboratory and field components. Total units is calculated automatically; this project retains 60 contact minutes per unit per week for every component. A 2-unit laboratory plus 1-unit lecture therefore requires 120 lab minutes and 60 lecture minutes. Components may use different days and compatible facilities, with one instructor for the complete subject. Existing subjects are migrated to one component based on their existing type; no lecture/lab breakdown is invented. Release affected committed schedules before changing an established component plan.

Requested facilities: Select a building and one or more exact rooms/labs/fields, then add another request group as needed. Requests constrain each matching component type. Components with no requested facility of their type use normal suitable facilities. A foreign requested facility still requires the borrowing flow. Manual shortage choices are explicit overrides for that preview, not silent changes to subject requests.

Wait and Force: Standard generation uses own/shared facilities first, majors before minors. A shortage identifies foreign candidates without reserving them. Wait waits for other active section-owning departments to commit complete schedules for that period, then generates a new continuation preview while preserving valid existing placements. It never commits for you. Catalog-only departments without active sections do not block the queue. Force requires confirmation before trying free suitable foreign facilities; it never displaces a committed meeting or ignores hard constraints. Borrowed meetings prefer 07:00-08:30, 11:30-13:00 and 17:30-19:00 before other open hours.

Generation review: The live log reports real selection/placement events and processed subject-section requirements. Section, Room and Faculty tabs open day-group timetable previews (MWF, TTh, optional Saturday and other actual days). Unresolved, Missing Adviser and Borrowed Room remain separate review tabs. Facility resolution shows laboratory, field and room candidate counts and current availability; a free component slot is not a guarantee that the whole weekly subject can be placed.

Print and email: Preview PDFs are marked as previews. Committed runs support printing and an explicit Send PDF dialog with selected resource scope and recipient confirmation. The backend requires SMTP_EMAIL and SMTP_PASS; optional SMTP_HOST and SMTP_PORT default to smtp.gmail.com and 587. The schedule sender requires STARTTLS and does not support implicit-TLS port 465. No email is sent automatically on commit or when Wait resumes.

Defaults and upgrade: Migration 9 changes exactly-45 room capacities and section student counts to 50 and sets new-record defaults to 50. Existing values such as 40 or 60 are preserved. It creates a backup first, retains IDs, weekly working hours, names and committed meetings, and adds separate component, facility-request and wait-queue tables. Older previews become stale and should be regenerated. The section bank remains reusable across semesters; unique section identity is program + year + code.

Safe deletion, relationship review and developer tools

Academic → Curricula / Academic periods: Open the delete action to preview precisely which sections, subject-year links, faculty teaching assignments and saved timetable runs rely on the record. Every related group has links to the corresponding management area or detail modal. Ordinary deletion remains blocked when relationships exist; an Administrator or Developer can explicitly select one of the safer resolution paths below.

  1. Unlink a relationship (safe default): Open a curriculum's subject list or a faculty profile, then choose Unlink beside the membership, eligible subject, or explicit teaching assignment. The original subject, faculty, curriculum, and section records stay. If a saved schedule or assignment would become invalid, unlinking is blocked until those dependencies are resolved. A section adviser can also be unlinked without deleting either person or section.
  2. Delete a standalone record: After removing its required links, choose Delete this record only. This deletes the selected original row, not its otherwise related records.
  3. Edit dependencies first: Follow the linked records to change their relationships and try deleting again. This keeps the remaining college records intact.
  4. Move sections, then delete: When another academic period or compatible curriculum exists, move the related sections there. Instructor links are preserved; saved timetable runs affected by the change are released. A replacement curriculum must already include all required subject-year mappings, or the transfer is rejected.
  5. Delete record and clear its links: Use this separately confirmed operation when deleting a parent whose references must be cleared. Independent rows are preserved: deleting a curriculum keeps the original subjects and sections and clears sections.curriculum_id; deleting an academic period clears sections.period_id; deleting a building leaves its rooms to be reassigned. Relation-only rows such as curriculum memberships and invalid instructor links are removed, and affected saved generation runs are released. Type DELETE AND UNLINK. An SQLite backup is created first; protected accounts are not deleted.

Normalized data structure: subjects stores subject details once. curriculum_subject_years stores only a curriculum ID, subject ID, year level, and source semester; faculty_subjects stores faculty–subject eligibility and teaching_assignments stores explicit instructor–subject–section choices. These links are not separate copies of the subject or faculty. Removing a curriculum membership may remove that subject from a section's required-subject list, but it never deletes the section; the impact preview identifies affected sections first.

SQLite relationship safety (schema migration 5)

Optional foreign keys now use ON DELETE SET NULL at the database level. For example, deleting a curriculum after its subject-year memberships have been reviewed and removed leaves the section row alive and sets sections.curriculum_id to NULL. The same principle applies to academic periods, programs, department ownership, advisers and room buildings. Original subject, faculty, section and room rows are never duplicates of their links.

Association tables such as curriculum_subject_years, faculty_subjects and teaching_assignments deliberately keep restrictive foreign keys; they must be unlinked explicitly. Saved generation runs and committed meetings are not silently cascade-deleted. SQLite triggers block direct changes to a section's academic context, curriculum membership or instructor eligibility while a committed meeting still relies on it. The reviewed deletion workflow first releases affected schedule runs, then detaches references and deletes the selected record. All changes are validated in a transaction and linked deletions create a database backup first.

New indexes support dependency lookups; an upgrade from an older database creates an additional pre-migration backup. Always back up your latest database.db before copying a project update and use the Delete and unlink action rather than raw SQL to clean linked records.

Developer → Database: Choose a table with the searchable dropdown, click/select a row to inspect its columns and referenced records, edit supported business fields, or open the same dependency-aware delete dialog. IDs and generated fields (created_at, updated_at, committed_at) cannot be edited; generation runs and meetings can be inspected and deleted as a unit, but not modified in place. Account/password/session/audit tables are deliberately excluded from CRUD.

Developer → System: Live host CPU/model/load, RAM, disk, GPU model (where detectable), internet probe status, SQLite integrity, Go process metrics and the latest audit events. Diagnostics are best-effort: GPU usage may not be reported, and a failed internet probe may mean firewall restrictions rather than a complete outage. This is an application activity log, not a full Windows Event Viewer log.

Before deleting an academic period: Choose Move sections & delete to preserve a valid period assignment, or Delete and unlink to retain the sections with Academic period: Unassigned. In either case, saved timetable runs that depend on the deleted period are released. Reassign orphaned sections before generating again.

  2. Extract the new ZIP into a different folder; do not blindly overwrite a running database.
  3. The included database is based on the SQLite file you uploaded. To preserve changes made after that upload, copy your latest database/database.db and root .env into the newly extracted folder before its first launch.
  4. Start the server from the extracted scheduler/server directory. The earlier schema is upgraded on startup. A consistent backup is created first under database/backups/.
  5. Open the workspace, sign in with your existing local account, and use Ctrl+F5 to load the updated frontend. Session tokens were cleared in the packaged database; your local account and password hash were retained.

Starting a genuinely empty workspace

Only after backing up and deliberately choosing an empty database path, use:

go run .

-seed reads database/seed.sql transactionally and refuses to run if any business data already exists. An empty database will then ask you to create a local administrator in the browser. Do not run the seed command to update an existing workspace.

3. Updated Academic and Faculty workflows

Academic

Sections is a dedicated call-to-action at the right of the academic tabs. Sections can be filtered by department, program, year level and academic period. Subjects can be filtered by department/program and curriculum year, including Unassigned.

In Academic → Curricula, use New curriculum to create a curriculum for a chosen program. Then use Add curriculum subjects to select department, program, curriculum, year level and existing subject records. Removing an obsolete curriculum does not delete its original subject or section rows; sections become Unassigned until relinked. Existing curriculum containers remain preserved unless you explicitly delete one. Curriculum details have a year filter.

Each section still resolves requirements from its selected curriculum and year. A section's adviser is selected through the faculty picker, independently of teaching. Its subject list offers an instructor picker restricted to faculty eligible for that subject. If a class already has an instructor, review/remove the existing assignment explicitly rather than silently replacing it.

Faculty assignment from Subjects

In Academic → Subjects, click the Faculty cell to open a searchable multi-select picker. Choose subject-eligible faculty by name, department or program. Saving changes eligibility for that subject and does not choose a section or create an adviser appointment. Faculty members attached to explicit teaching assignments or committed meetings remain protected until the relevant schedule or explicit assignment is deliberately released.

Explicit and automatic instructor choices

In Faculty / Sections, choose Priority (selected sections first, other eligible sections allowed) or Strict (only saved sections). Each eligible subject has a Select sections button. The child dialog lists active sections linked through that subject's curriculum and year. Child selections remain drafts until the parent Save sections succeeds; canceling the parent does not save. Eligibility and adviser assignment remain separate. Generation previews never become permanent explicit teaching assignments.

Facilities organized by building

Facilities now lists each building or field area first, with its rooms and laboratories nested underneath. Expand or collapse a building and add or edit rooms from that parent card. Room ownership is independent of the current department using it: force mode can borrow vacant, suitable rooms without erasing a previously committed class.

Appearance and college branding

The attached Buenavista Community College seal appears in workspace, access, guide, browser icon and print preview. Toggle Light mode or Dark mode in the header (or the access screen). Both themes have their own surface, text, emphasis, warning, danger, border and hover colors; preferences persist on the same browser. Printing remains optimized for a white page. The interface uses muted college-green accents on a light or comfortable, low-glare dark background. Each theme has its own text, surface, dialog, input and feedback colors.

Searchable selection dialogs

Use the status filter to display all records, already selected records, or only records not yet selected. Selection is maintained across year/department/program filters and pages.

Faculty/adviser inputs open a name, department and program picker. Program relationships are derived from eligible subjects and teaching assignments, not invented profile fields. Subject inputs open a code/name, department, program and year picker. Multi-selection stays selected as filters or pages change; already assigned faculty subjects remain locked until their teaching assignments are removed.

Faculty

Subjects: click the cell to add/remove subject eligibility. Sections: choose one of that faculty member's eligible subjects and then compatible sections. Availability: click to edit working days/hours, including Sunday and Saturday. Consideration: toggle priority placement through confirmation. Status: activate/deactivate through confirmation. The Actions column retains edit/delete rather than separate Profile and clock buttons.

Section compatibility requires the subject to be present in both the faculty's eligibility list and the section's selected curriculum/year. Existing instructor assignments remain exclusive. An adviser still does not need to teach the section and does not consume teaching hours.

4. Scheduling priorities and subject meeting days

  1. Resolve the selected department's sections and required subjects. Report missing subject eligibility and faculty availability; do not fabricate teachers.
  2. Place major subjects before minor subjects. Within each group, explicit assignments and consideration preferences guide selection, with seeded tie ordering. An explicit instructor is never silently replaced.
  3. Enforce selected subject days, exact contact minutes, assigned instructor eligibility, faculty working hours/unavailable windows, room suitability, capacity and all room/faculty/section conflicts.
  4. Search departmental/shared rooms and selected regular days first. Force mode may borrow free rooms from other departments but never displace committed reservations. The engine tests the finite room/day/time candidates rather than reporting an arbitrary SEARCH_BUDGET cutoff. Any remaining request timeout is a genuine request-level safety limit and must not be presented as a faculty/room shortage.
  5. Only after primary-day room candidates fail, try Saturday fallback for a subject that explicitly enables it. This includes checking all primary-day shared/borrowable rooms first, instead of choosing Saturday merely to retain an own-department room.
  6. Keep valid partial results, show every unresolved requirement, and revalidate in one write transaction before commit. Previously saved stale previews must be regenerated.

Shortage codes and actions

MISSING_INSTRUCTOR means no eligible active faculty was linked to the subject: open Academic → Subjects → Faculty and select capable faculty. LACK_INSTRUCTOR or BREAK_CONFLICT means eligible faculty exist but suitable complete weekly times were not found: review working hours, lunch and existing meetings; the Resolve action allows an explicitly chosen eligible instructor without changing all section assignments. LACK_OF_FACILITY means a compatible vacant room was not available for the requested meeting combinations: inspect the facilities and try force mode if borrowing is appropriate. SECTION_CONFLICT means the section has collisions or too many consecutive meetings; check its current schedule. EMPTY_CURRICULUM means the section has no mapped subjects for its selected curriculum/year: Resolve opens the curriculum. These reasons describe the dominant rejected candidate category, not an exhaustive optimization proof.

Sunday through Saturday

Each subject has seven checkboxes, displayed Sunday first. New subjects default to Monday-Friday. Checking only Sunday makes it a Sunday-only subject; checking only Saturday makes Saturday mandatory. A separately enabled Saturday fallback does not make it a regular day and never enables Sunday. It is optional, usually appropriate for minors. A checked regular Saturday disables the redundant fallback setting.

Faculty must also work on the chosen weekend day. There is no academic-period Saturday switch. The source Saturday-only settings are retained, but subject names such as NSTP, ROTC or DEFTACS do not automatically override the dump's actual settings. Set their checkboxes deliberately to reflect your policy.

Fixed patterns such as MWF, TTh, Saturday and Sunday must be compatible with selected days. Automatic/flexible placement tries bounded day combinations while preserving exact contact minutes. Each meeting remains limited to 180 minutes in this engine; a longer single-day requirement is reported as unplaceable rather than truncated.

6. Boundaries and decisions kept visible

  • Local, LAN and HTTPS-tunnel access are supported; Developer, Administrator and department-scoped Scheduler login remain enforced. Restrict public access with an identity-aware tunnel and a security/privacy review. PostgreSQL migration is not implemented.
  • Semester/term remain scheduling context, not automatic curriculum subject filters. Subject year means curriculum year level, not academic-year date.
  • Existing curriculum containers are retained. A Developer or Administrator can create a new curriculum, attach existing subjects through the curriculum/year membership table, and reassign sections whose curriculum link was cleared. Imported labels are displayed more clearly without silently rewriting their original audit values.
  • Co-teaching is not implemented. Automatic selection is limited to eligible faculty and does not permanently create an explicit section assignment. Explicit instructors are never silently swapped. Unassigned requirements may use available subject-eligible faculty, without creating forced links; genuine shortages remain reviewable.
  • Working hours are hard constraints; the consideration flag is an ordering preference. Source written preferences are notes, not automatically enforced time rules.
  • Stored historical preview labels may contain the earlier anonymous text. Display/print substitutes current faculty names for those known placeholders; the old preview's placements remain historical and must be regenerated after the data revision.
  • Previously committed meetings block direct resource changes that could invalidate them. Release/regenerate the affected schedule deliberately. Upgrades do not automatically change working hours for already committed faculty.
  • Weekly repetition, 15-minute resolution and finite candidate search are retained. Holidays, exam-specific exceptions, unrestricted long single-day classes and exhaustive global optimization are not implemented.
